- **This repository does not itself assert or establish any exclusive
  right over the underlying method, technique, or combination of
  pedagogical/AI-tutoring mechanisms described in the published works.**
  Copyright protection asserted for the published works concerns their
  protectable expression, not the underlying ideas, procedures, systems,
  or methods — consistent with standard copyright doctrine (see e.g. the
  U.S. Copyright Office's general FAQ, and WIPO's overview of copyright
  scope).
- No claim of "worldwide novelty." The Prior-art Note v1.0
  (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21935637) documents a *bounded, moderate-confidence*
  non-finding within a specific reviewed source set — not an assertion that
  no similar work exists anywhere.
- No claim that internal dates inside the private evidence archive are
  independently verified. The public commitment fixes the archive's content
  against later alteration as of its publication date — it does not
  independently timestamp every date that appears inside it.
