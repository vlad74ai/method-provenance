## Scope

This public commitment identifies one fixed private evidence selection relating to the historical development and prepublication formalization of Method Definition v1.0.

The selected archive contains seven short historical excerpts and private metadata sufficient to locate and compare their exact source revisions. It does not contain complete product concepts, full course materials, or complete pilot logs.

The private archive is **not published** here. Internal source filenames, repository paths, personal information, instructional text, and product design details are deliberately withheld.

## Meaning and limits

This hash permits a later disclosed copy of the private ZIP to be checked against the exact bytes publicly committed here.

The record documents the existence of that selection from the date it became publicly visible; it **does not** independently verify when any underlying idea was conceived, authorship of each historic sentence, or the reliability of dates appearing in source documents. Git commit metadata provides a traceable version history, not independent certification of each idea's origin.

This is neither a claim of worldwide novelty or exclusive rights to broad educational methods, nor experimental evidence of the method's effectiveness.
