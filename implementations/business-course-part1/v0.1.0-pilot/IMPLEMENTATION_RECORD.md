## Relationship to Method Definition v1.0

A structural review of the versioned Part 1 package found course materials corresponding to all six functions described by the Method Definition:

1. **Author-governed instructional specification:** A defined module sequence, learning prerequisites, and module boundaries.
2. **Instruction-programmed AI teacher:** The course brief and instructions establish a teaching role for a general-purpose conversational model.
3. **Runtime micro-adaptation:** Teaching instructions permit explanatory depth and examples to adapt to learner context and preferences.
4. **Learner steering:** Learners are taught to change the form and depth of explanations by directing the model in dialogue.
5. **Evidence-based checking and remediation:** Module checks request observable actions and direct the learner toward relevant earlier content when difficulties arise.
6. **Transfer:** Course practice invites applying learned ways of working to the learner's own tasks, rather than only scripted examples.

This mapping attests to the **documented structure** of this particular package. It is not a controlled study of teaching effectiveness or a guarantee that all six functions operate reliably in every model or learner session.
